## Phase Goal

**Source:** `.planning/ROADMAP.md` Phase 3 `**Goal:**` line, quoted verbatim —

> A fan landing on darlng.com encounters the full cinematic hero for Eseriani with embed player and streaming CTAs, can browse the back-catalog discography, navigate to a per-release listen-everywhere page with branded platform buttons, and follow DARLNG on all social platforms.

> **MVP_MODE note:** the ROADMAP Goal line for Phase 3 is not in `As a / I want to / so that` user-story form (nor were Phases 1 and 2, which shipped). Per the MVP-mode contract a user story is **not invented here**. To convert it, run `/gsd-mvp-phase 3` before planning. The goal above is used as written.

<objective>
Build the spine of the fan experience end-to-end: a fan lands on `/`, sees the Eseriani cover art full-bleed with the release title and three streaming CTAs, and can click straight through to `/listen/eseriani` where every configured platform gets its own prominent branded button. `getStaticPaths()` gives the other three releases their listen pages in the same move.

This is the **tracer** for Phase 3 — it wires every layer the phase touches (design tokens → typed data → icon lookup → component → page route → built HTML → browser) along one real path, at production quality. The facade (Plan 02) and the discography grid (Plan 03) expand outward from this proven slice.

Purpose: HERO-01 and LISTEN-01, and the architectural proof that a full-bleed hero composes correctly inside Phase 2's container-constrained `Layout.astro` without horizontal overflow.
Output: `platform-icons.ts`, `PlatformButton.astro`, `listen/[slug].astro`, a rewritten `index.astro` hero, three additions to `global.css`, and browser evidence at 375/768/1440.
</objective>
