# Phase 3 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the hero, discography grid, and `/listen/[slug]` pages —
> the site's defining visual moment. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher (autonomous run per
> `03-CONTEXT.md`'s "you pick, make it pop" delegation). **This spec INHERITS Phase 2's design
> system (`02-UI-SPEC.md`) exactly — colors, fonts, spacing scale, button base contract, and
> interaction states are not redefined here.** It ADDS the page-level contracts Phase 2
> explicitly deferred: the fluid hero display size, hero composition, facade play mechanic,
> discography card anatomy, listen-page layout, and the platform icon mapping table.

### Scrim & contrast rule (the one new "color pair" this phase introduces) — CORRECTED 2026-08-08 post-checker BLOCK

The hero renders text over photographic imagery, not a flat surface — this is the one place a new "pair" could sneak in unverified. The rule locked here: **text never sits directly on raw image pixels.** It always sits inside a scrim zone where the composited background luminance is dominated by `--color-bg` at ≥85% opacity through the *entire* declared content band, verified below with explicit interpolated math — not eyeballed.

**Original stops were wrong** (`0%→95%, 30%→88%, 55%→45%, 78%→transparent`): interpolating between the 30% and 55% stops crosses the 85%-opacity floor at h≈31.7%, meaning the ≥85% zone was really only the bottom ~32% of the hero, not the ~45% content band the prior draft claimed. At the 55% stop (45% opacity), `#F5F1EA` over a worst-case white image patch dropped to ~2.8:1 — a hard AA failure if a wrapped mobile CTA row or the facade panel's top edge reached that height.

**Corrected stops** (tightened per the checker's option (b) — widen the ≥85% zone rather than shrink the content band):

```css
/* Hero scrim — applied as an absolutely-positioned overlay above the art, below the content */
background: linear-gradient(
  to top,
  color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-bg) 95%, transparent) 0%,
  color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-bg) 88%, transparent) 50%,
  color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-bg) 40%, transparent) 72%,
  transparent 92%
);
```

**Recomputed interpolated opacity at every requested height**, plus the resulting contrast ratio for each hero text color against the WORST CASE underlying pixel (pure white, `#FFFFFF`, the lightest plausible tone in the Eseriani sky) — methodology: linearly interpolate the gradient's declared opacity between its bracketing stops at each height, alpha-composite `--color-bg` (`#0A0908`) over `#FFFFFF` in encoded sRGB at that opacity, then apply the WCAG relative-luminance contrast formula (verified against Phase 2's own flat-pair numbers as a sanity check — e.g. `#F5F1EA` on 100%-opacity `#0A0908` reproduces Phase 2's exact 17.67:1):

| Height (from bottom) | Interpolated opacity | Composite vs `#FFFFFF` — `#F5F1EA` contrast | `#2DD9C5` (accent) contrast | `#8C8378` (muted) contrast |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% (stop) | 95.0% | **16.16:1** ✅ | — | — |
| 30% | 90.8% | **14.51:1** ✅ | — | 4.38:1 ⚠ (see fix below) |
| 45% (content-band edge) | 88.7% | **13.63:1** ✅ | 8.65:1 ✅ | 4.11:1 ❌ (see fix below) |
| 50% (stop, safety-margin edge) | 88.0% | **13.33:1** ✅ | — | — |
| 55% (past the safety margin) | 77.1% | **9.00:1** ✅ | — | — |
| 72% (stop, outside content band — art-only zone) | 40.0% | **2.42:1** ❌ (expected/OK — no text ever renders this high) | — | — |

Every height inside the declared content band (0–45%, with a 50% safety margin per the checker's request) clears **13.6:1 – 16.2:1** for `#F5F1EA`, comfortably above the 4.5:1 floor, against the worst plausible underlying pixel — not just a typical one. `#2DD9C5` (the hero kicker) clears 8.65:1 at the content-band edge (its most conservative point in-band), also safe.

**Finding during recomputation — `--color-text-muted` does NOT clear the bar inside the scrim, even with the corrected gradient.** `#8C8378`'s own luminance (0.232) is low enough that it needs the composite background at ≥~92% opacity to hold 4.5:1 against a worst-case white patch — tighter than the 88% floor this gradient guarantees through the content band. At 30% and 45% height it lands at 4.38:1 and 4.11:1 — both **fail** AA for normal-size text. Phase 2 verified `--color-text-muted` at 5.34–4.95:1 only for its two ORIGINAL flat-surface pairs (on `--color-bg`, on `--color-surface`) — that verification does not extend to a photographic scrim, and this phase's original draft incorrectly assumed it would.

**Fix:** the hero artist line ("Darlng x Tobiko") switches from `--color-text-muted` to `--color-text` (see Hero Composition structure below, updated) — `--color-text` clears 13:1+ throughout the content band with the same margin as the headline. `--color-text-muted` remains correctly reserved for its Phase-2-verified flat-surface contexts only (cards, footer, listen-page meta line on flat `--color-bg`) and is never used inside a scrim zone anywhere in this phase's scope.

**Visual consequence check — does the tighter gradient swallow the artwork's face?** No, and here's the math, not just an assertion: `object-position: 35% 28%` anchors the face at roughly 28% down from the top of the hero container, i.e. ~72% up from the bottom — which lands almost exactly on the new gradient's 72% stop (40% opacity). That's a real, visible increase in darkening at the face compared to the original (miscalculated) gradient, which was nearly clear (~12% opacity) at that same height. A 40% neutral-dark tint across the face is moderate, not obscuring — the face remains clearly legible, and given the project's explicit "dark, moody, cinematic" brand direction (CONTEXT.md, BRAND-01), it reads as intentional mood-setting rather than a defect. **`object-position: 35% 28%` is retained unchanged** — no adjustment needed, but this consequence is now stated explicitly rather than left implicit.

**This remains a visual-QA item, not something `check-contrast.mjs` can test** (that script verifies flat token pairs, not image composites) — the corrected safe-zone boundary and required screenshot check are documented in UI Considerations below.

## Hero Composition (HERO-01, HERO-02)

**Decision: full-bleed background art with gradient scrim + bottom-anchored content** (not a framed/boxed art treatment) — maximizes the cinematic feeling the brief asks for, and the scrim rule above guarantees AA-safe text regardless of the underlying image's warm/light tones.

### Structure (applies at all breakpoints unless noted)

```
<section class="hero"> (full-bleed, min-h-svh mobile / lg:min-h-[85vh])
  ├─ <Picture> — Eseriani cover art, absolute inset-0, object-cover, object-position: 35% 28%
  │    (THE LCP element — eager, fetchpriority="high", no lazy)
  ├─ scrim overlay (gradient above)
  └─ content zone (container-constrained, flex, justify-end / items-end — bottom-anchored)
       ├─ kicker: "OUT NOW" (14px, Manrope 600, uppercase, tracking-wide, accent)
       ├─ h1: "Eseriani" (text-hero, Unbounded 800, leading-[1.05], --color-text)
       ├─ artist line: "Darlng x Tobiko" (16px, Manrope 600, --color-text — NOT --color-text-muted; see Scrim & contrast rule fix above)
       ├─ CTA row: Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube pills + "Listen everywhere →" link
       └─ facade panel (16:9, contained — see Facade Mechanic Contract)
```

### object-position lock

`object-position: 35% 28%` (single value, no breakpoint variation) — keeps the woman's face (the art's primary human subject, roughly centered at x:20–45%, y:25–55% of the 1254×1254 source) fully in-frame at every tested crop ratio from portrait (375×~700 effective hero height) through the wider desktop crop (1440×~700). The baked-in "DARLNG x TOBIKO / Eseriani" title text on the right side of the source art will partially crop out at narrow/tall ratios — intentional and acceptable, since the real, accessible, SEO-crawlable HTML `<h1>` replaces it; the baked-in art text is decorative background at that point, not the a11y source of truth.

### Per-breakpoint layout

**375px (mobile):**
- `min-height: 100svh` (falls back to `100vh` if `svh` unsupported)
- Content zone: `px-4 pb-12`, `flex flex-col gap-2` (kicker→h1), `mt-4` (h1→artist line), `mt-8` (artist line→CTA row), `mt-8` (CTA row→facade panel)
- CTA row: `flex flex-wrap gap-2` — 3 pills wrap to 2 rows if needed at 343px content width; never truncate a label
- Facade panel: full content width, `aspect-video`, `rounded-[--radius-card]` — likely renders partially below the fold on short devices (iPhone SE ~667px tall); this is accepted (see Facade section) since the CTA row (the "play/save/follow in one move" moment) stays above the fold, not the embed itself

**768px (tablet):**
- `min-height: 90svh`
- Content zone: `px-6 pb-16`, same vertical stack, CTA row `flex gap-3` (fits inline without wrap at this width)
- Facade panel: `max-w-xl` (480px), still stacked below CTA row (single-column content zone persists through this breakpoint)

**1440px (desktop, `lg:` and up):**
- `min-height: 85vh`, container caps at 1280px per Phase 2's container contract (extra `--color-bg` on either side reads as intentional, consistent with Phase 2's stated 1440px behavior)
- Content zone becomes a two-column grid: `lg:grid lg:grid-cols-[1.1fr_1fr] lg:gap-12 lg:items-end`
  - **Left column:** kicker → h1 → artist line → CTA row → "Listen everywhere →" link
  - **Right column:** facade panel, `lg:max-w-lg` (512px), vertically aligned to the bottom of the left column's CTA row
- Both columns bottom-anchor via `items-end`, `pb-16`

### CTA row anatomy

Three streaming buttons — **Secondary/ghost variant from Phase 2's button contract** (transparent bg, accent border+text, `rounded-full`, `py-3 px-6`), each with a `BrandIcon` (Spotify/Apple Music/YouTube — all three have verified `simple-icons` marks) at 18px + label, `gap-2` between icon and label internally, `44px` minimum height satisfied by the `py-3` padding. `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` (external, matches the security-audited invariant from Phase 2).

"Listen everywhere →" — plain text link, not a button: `--color-accent` text, `ChevronRight` (lucide, 16px) suffix, underline-on-hover, `href="/listen/eseriani"` (internal, no `target="_blank"`).

### Facade Mechanic Contract (HERO-02, zero-JS-until-click)

**Placement decision: in-hero, contained panel** (not full-bleed, not below-the-fold as a separate section) — sits within the hero's content flow as documented above. This is a *second*, smaller local image distinct from the full-bleed background LCP image — necessary because it needs its own 16:9 crop + play-button overlay treatment, but it stays far smaller in rendered area than the full-bleed background, so the background remains the LCP element by area (browser LCP selection picks the largest painted content element in the viewport).

**Pre-click state (static, zero JS, zero third-party requests):**
- Container: `aspect-video` (16:9), `rounded-[--radius-card]` (8px), `overflow-hidden`, `relative`
- Background: `<Picture>` sourced from `src/assets/releases/eseriani.jpg` (the SAME local asset, Sharp-cropped to 16:9 via `fit="cover"` — no new asset needed, no `i.ytimg.com` request), `loading="lazy"` (below the CTA row in DOM/paint priority), `formats={['avif','webp']}`
- Overlay tint: `background: color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-bg) 35%, transparent)` (uniform, lighter than the hero scrim — just enough to make the play button pop and visually signal "this is clickable video," not another static cover repeat)
- Play button: `<button type="button" aria-label="Play {youtubeEmbed.titleForA11y}">` absolutely centered
  - Size: **72px diameter (mobile) / 88px diameter (`lg:`)** — both comfortably exceed the 44px minimum
  - Default: `bg-accent` filled circle, `--color-bg` play-triangle icon (custom inline SVG or `@lucide/astro` `Play`, translated `+2px` on the x-axis for optical centering), icon size 28px mobile / 34px desktop
  - Hover: `scale-105`, glow — `box-shadow: 0 0 32px color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-accent) 50%, transparent), 0 0 64px color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-accent) 25%, transparent)`, 200ms ease — deliberately bigger/richer than Phase 2's 12px icon-hover glow (this is the hero's single most important click target, "premium... not a generic YouTube red triangle clone" per CONTEXT)
  - Focus-visible: `outline: 2px solid var(--color-accent); outline-offset: 4px` (larger offset than the system default 2px, since it's circular and the outline needs breathing room from the button edge)
  - Active/pressed: `scale-95` (matches system-wide pattern)

**Click behavior (plain `<script>`, not a Preact island):**
1. A scoped inline `<script>` at the bottom of the facade component queries `[data-facade-trigger]`.
2. On click: `document.createElement('iframe')`, set `src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/{videoId}?autoplay=1"`, `title="{titleForA11y}"`, `allow="autoplay; encrypted-media; picture-in-picture"`, `allowfullscreen`, `class` matching the container's fill (`absolute inset-0 w-full h-full border-0`).
3. Replace the thumbnail `<Picture>` + button node with the iframe **inside the same fixed 16:9 container** — dimensions never change, zero CLS.
4. No network request, no cookie, no localStorage write happens before this click (confirms CONTEXT's stated constraint).

**Error backstop:** if the iframe fails to load post-click (ad-blocker, network issue), no in-app retry/fallback UI is defined for the embed itself — the always-visible hero CTA row's direct YouTube link (`releases[0].platforms` entry) remains the working escape hatch. See UI Considerations.

## Discography (MUSIC-01, MUSIC-02)

**Heading:** `<h2>The Catalog</h2>` — short, confident, avoids the slightly dry "Discography" default while staying instantly clear. Section padding: `py-16 md:py-16` (the 3xl/64px token, top and bottom), full container-constrained (not full-bleed).

**Grid:** `grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3 gap-8` — single column below `md:` (768px), 3-up from `md:` onward (verified fit at 768px: 720px content width, 2×24px gaps, 224px per card — workable for square art + 2 lines of text below).

### Card anatomy (one `<article>` per release, exactly 3 — Randevu, Brave, Open Wide, in `releases.ts` order excluding `isLatest`)

```
<article>
  ├─ <a href="/listen/{slug}" aria-label="{title} — listen and platforms">
  │    ├─ <Picture> — cover art, aspect-square, rounded-[--radius-card], object-cover
  │    │    Sharp widths [300,400,600,800], sizes="(min-width:1280px) 384px, (min-width:768px) 224px, 100vw"
  │    │    loading="lazy" (below the fold)
  │    └─ <h3>{title}</h3> — 24px Unbounded 700 (reuses Subheading role)
  ├─ <p>{year} · {featureCredit line, e.g. "ft. Shubi Di Badman"}</p> — 14px Manrope 400, --color-text-muted
  ├─ platform icon row — first 3 entries of platforms[] in array order, icon-only
  │    each: 44×44px tap target (32px visual glyph + padding), BrandIcon or ExternalLink fallback
  │    target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer", aria-label="Listen to {title} on {platform label}"
  └─ <a href="/listen/{slug}" aria-label="All {title} platforms">All platforms →</a>
       14px Manrope 600, --color-accent, ChevronRight suffix
</article>
```

**Two links to the same `/listen/{slug}` destination is intentional** (art+title, and the explicit "All platforms →" line) — a standard, well-understood card pattern (art is the primary click target for browsing users; the text link is the explicit affordance for keyboard/screen-reader users scanning link text). Distinguished via `aria-label` so they don't read as duplicate ambiguous "Randevu" / "Randevu" links to assistive tech.

**No year/genre chips** — Claude's discretion, declined: keeps the card visually quiet relative to the hero, avoids competing decoration. Year is inline text with the feature credit instead.

**Hover state (desktop, pointer only):** cover art `scale-105`, card gets `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`, title text color transitions to `--color-accent` — all 200ms ease, all on the combined art+title link (not on the whole `<article>`, since the icon row and "All platforms" link have their own independent hover states per the system-wide link contract from Phase 2).
